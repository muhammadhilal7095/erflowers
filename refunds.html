We want every Envie Roses order to be perfect. If something isn’t right, we will always do our best to put it right quickly. This policy does not affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Fresh flowers and change of mind

Because fresh flowers are perishable, the usual 14-day right to cancel online purchases does not apply to them (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013). This means we cannot accept returns or offer refunds if you simply change your mind after your order has been prepared or delivered. Bespoke and personalised arrangements are also excluded.

Changing or cancelling an order

  • Contact us as soon as possible on WhatsApp or by email with your order reference.
  • If we have not yet started preparing your order, we will cancel it and refund you in full, or change the delivery date, address or card message for you.
  • Once your order has been prepared or dispatched, we are unable to cancel it, but we will always try to help.

For changes, please let us know as early as possible, ideally at least 48 hours before your delivery date.

If something is wrong with your order

If your roses arrive damaged, are not as described or are of unsatisfactory quality, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with:

  • your order reference (for example ER-261025-AB12);
  • a clear photo of the arrangement and packaging;
  • a short description of the problem.

We will then offer a replacement or a full or partial refund, depending on the issue. Please keep the flowers and packaging until we have resolved your request.

Late or failed deliveries

If we fail to deliver on the agreed date because of an error on our part, we will offer a redelivery or a refund. We cannot offer a refund where a delivery fails because of an incorrect or incomplete address, or because the recipient was unavailable and no safe place or instructions were given, although we will try to arrange a redelivery.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are made to your original payment method within 14 days of us agreeing the refund. Card refunds can take a few working days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.
