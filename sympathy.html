Flowers can say what words often can’t. Our sympathy roses are arranged with care in calm, gentle tones, to send to a home or to the funeral.

Sending flowers to a funeral

  • Ask the family or funeral director first: some families prefer donations instead of flowers.
  • For a funeral, send flowers to the funeral director the day before the service. Put the funeral director’s name and address as the delivery address, and the name of the person who has died plus the date of the service in the notes.
  • For a home, a bouquet or rose box a few days after the funeral is often appreciated, when the house is quieter.
