Questions

How does the free trial work?

How do I get my member discount?

Log in to your account before you check out. Your discount comes off the flowers in your bag automatically, and delivery is free when your order qualifies. Member discounts can’t be added to discount codes: we automatically use whichever saves you more. Gifts and add-ons aren’t discounted.

What does “free standard delivery” mean?

Standard delivery on your chosen date is free when your order meets your plan’s minimum (VIP: always). Same-day delivery is charged as normal.

How does the VIP monthly bouquet work?

For each paid month we prepare one standard bouquet for you, chosen by our florists. We’ll message you to confirm the delivery date and address. The monthly bouquet starts with your first paid month, not during the free trial.

How do I cancel?

Go to My Account → Membership and press Cancel membership, or message us. Your benefits continue until the end of the period you’ve paid for (or the end of your trial), and you won’t be charged again.

Can I change plan?

Yes. Message us and we’ll switch you over, or cancel and join the new plan when your current period ends.

Membership terms

  • Membership is a monthly subscription that renews automatically each month at the plan price shown until you cancel.
  • New members get a free trial of the length shown. One free trial per customer. If you don’t cancel before the trial ends, the monthly price is charged on the date shown when you join, and then every month on the same day.
  • You can cancel at any time in My Account or by contacting us. Cancelling stops future payments; your benefits continue until the end of the current paid period or trial.
  • Member discounts apply to flowers (not gifts, add-ons, delivery or gift cards), can’t be combined with discount codes, and apply only while you’re logged in with an active membership.
  • We’ll tell you in advance of any price change, and you can cancel before it applies.
  • These terms don’t affect your statutory rights. See our Terms & Conditions.
