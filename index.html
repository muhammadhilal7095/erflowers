Rose Care Guide

Make the Moment Last

A little care goes a long way. Follow these simple steps to keep your fresh roses looking their most beautiful for longer.

Ask Our Florists
Trim the stems

Cut 2–3 cm from each stem at an angle with a clean, sharp knife or scissors before placing roses in water.

Fresh water, often

Use a clean vase and replace the water every two days, trimming the stems again each time.

Remove lower leaves

Strip any leaves that would sit below the waterline to keep the water clean for longer.

Choose the right spot

Keep your roses away from direct sunlight, radiators and fruit bowls, as heat and ripening fruit shorten their life.
