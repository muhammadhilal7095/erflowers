Questions & Answers

If I order today, can my roses arrive today?

Yes, in selected areas. Order by (UK time) and choose today’s date at checkout. Enter the postcode above to see if same-day delivery is available there.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. For some islands and remote areas, please message us first so we can confirm timings.

Can I choose the delivery date and time?

Yes. Pick any available date at checkout, plus a morning, afternoon or evening slot. We aim to deliver within the slot you choose.

Can I add a card message?

Yes, a personal card message is included free with every order. Write it at checkout.

What if the recipient isn’t in?

We may leave the roses with a neighbour or in a safe, sheltered place, or arrange another delivery. Add a safe place or the recipient’s phone number at checkout to help us deliver first time.

How do I pay?

Send your order on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm payment with you, or pay securely by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay at checkout.

What if my roses aren’t perfect?

Tell us within 24 hours with a photo and we’ll replace them or give you a refund. See our Freshness Guarantee.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Use Track Your Order with your order reference and postcode, or log in to My Account.

Do you sell gift cards and subscriptions?

Yes. Send a gift card, or join the Flower Club for regular deliveries you can pause or cancel any time.
