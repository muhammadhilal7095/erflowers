How it works

  • Tell us about your day: the date, venue, colours and roughly what you need. Photos and Pinterest boards are welcome.
  • Your proposal: we suggest designs and a clear quote, with no obligation.
  • Secure your date: a deposit holds your date. We finalise numbers and details closer to the day.
  • On the day: we deliver to you and your venue, on time and ready to go.

We recommend getting in touch 3 to 6 months before your wedding, especially for spring and summer dates. Short notice? Ask anyway; we’ll always try.
