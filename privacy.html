This policy explains how (“we”, “us”) collects and uses your personal data when you visit our website, place an order or contact us. We are committed to protecting your privacy in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

We are the controller of your personal data. You can contact us about this policy or your data at .

2. The information we collect

  • Your details: your name, phone number and email address when you place an order or contact us.
  • Recipient details: the recipient’s name, delivery address and (if you provide it) phone number.
  • Order details: the products you order, delivery date, card message and any notes.
  • Messages: anything you send us by WhatsApp, email or phone.
  • Newsletter: your email address if you sign up for updates.

Card payments are processed by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not see or store your full card details.

4. Who we share it with

We only share your information where needed to run our business, with:

  • Stripe, to process card payments securely;
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to contact us or send your order via WhatsApp;
  • delivery partners, to deliver your order;
  • our website hosting and email providers, who store data on our behalf;
  • professional advisers (such as accountants) and authorities where required by law.

We never sell your personal data.

5. International transfers

Some of our providers, such as Stripe and Meta, may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on UK adequacy regulations (including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or approved safeguards such as the International Data Transfer Agreement.

6. How long we keep it

  • Order and payment records: up to six years, to meet HMRC tax and accounting requirements.
  • Customer service messages: as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

Your bag and order history on this website are stored in your own browser and stay there until you clear your browser data. See our Cookie Policy.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • ask us to restrict or object to how we use your data;
  • ask for your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent to marketing at any time.

To use any of these rights, email . We will respond within one month.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first so we can help. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
