Partner terms

  • You earn £10 for each order placed through your link within 30 days of the customer clicking it. If they clicked another partner’s link more recently, that partner gets the order.
  • Commission is earned when the order is delivered. Cancelled or refunded orders don’t earn commission. Gift card purchases aren’t included.
  • You can’t earn from your own orders or orders you place for others.
  • We pay by bank transfer once your earnings reach £50, usually at the start of each month.
  • You must make it clear that you earn from your link, for example with #ad or “affiliate link” on every post, as required by UK advertising rules (the CAP Code and the Competition and Markets Authority’s guidance).
  • Don’t make claims about our products that aren’t true, use spam, or bid on “Envie Roses” in paid search ads.
  • We may decline applications, and may close a partner account and withhold commission that comes from misleading promotion or misuse.
  • You’re responsible for declaring your earnings to HMRC if needed.
