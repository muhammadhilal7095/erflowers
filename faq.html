Ordering

How do I place an order?

Choose your roses, select a size and add them to your bag. At checkout, enter your details, the delivery address and date, and an optional card message. You can then pay securely by card or send your order to us on WhatsApp.

Can I order on WhatsApp?

Yes. Choose “Order on WhatsApp” at checkout and your full order will open in WhatsApp, ready to send. We will reply to confirm your order and payment. You can also simply message us to order or ask a question.

Can I send roses to someone else?

Of course. Most of our orders are gifts. Enter the recipient’s name and address in the delivery section at checkout.

Can I add a personal message?

Yes. Write your message at checkout (up to 250 characters) and we will include it on a card with your roses.

Payment

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept major debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay through our secure payment provider, Stripe. If you order on WhatsApp, we will send you a secure payment link or our bank details.

Is it safe to pay on your website?

Yes. Card payments are processed by Stripe on a secure, encrypted page. We never see or store your full card details.

Which currency are your prices in?

All prices are in pounds sterling (GBP).

Delivery

Where do you deliver?

We deliver to addresses across the UK. Please message us before ordering for the Scottish Highlands and Islands, the Isle of Man, the Isles of Scilly and the Channel Islands. See Delivery Information.

Do you offer same day delivery?

Yes. Call us or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll create a bouquet to suit your budget and deliver it today.

Can I choose a delivery time?

You choose the delivery date at checkout. We cannot guarantee a specific time unless agreed in advance, so if timing matters, add a note or message us first.

What if the recipient is not at home?

We may leave the roses with a neighbour or in a safe place, or arrange another delivery. Add any instructions in the notes at checkout.

Our Roses

Will my roses look exactly like the photos?

Roses are natural products, so every arrangement is unique. Colours and sizes may vary slightly, and occasionally we may substitute a container or flower of equal or greater value in the same style.

How long will my roses last?

Fresh roses typically last around a week with good care. Trim the stems, change the water every two days and keep them away from heat and direct sunlight.

Do you create bespoke arrangements?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your idea, colours and budget, and we will design something made only for you.

Changes & Refunds

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. If we have not started preparing your order, we can usually change or cancel it. See our Refunds & Returns policy.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order reference and a photo, and we will put it right with a replacement or refund.
