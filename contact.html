FAQ

How can I track my order?

Use Track Your Order with your order reference and delivery postcode. If you have an account, all your orders are in My Account. Can’t find your reference? Message us and we’ll check for you.

What delivery options do you offer, and how fast?

We deliver across the UK on the date you choose, with morning, afternoon or evening slots. Same-day delivery is available in selected areas when you order by . See Delivery Information.

Can I change the delivery address or date after ordering?

Yes, if we haven’t started preparing your roses. Message us as soon as possible with your order reference and the new details.

What does your freshness guarantee cover?

If your roses arrive damaged, aren’t as described or aren’t fresh, tell us within 24 hours with a photo and we’ll replace them or refund you. See our Freshness Guarantee.

What payment methods do you accept, and can I use a code?

Order on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm payment with you, or pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay at checkout. Discount codes and gift cards go in the code box at checkout.

How does the Flower Club work, and how do I cancel?

Choose a plan and how often you’d like your roses; delivery is included. You can pause or cancel any time from My Account or by messaging us. See the Flower Club.
