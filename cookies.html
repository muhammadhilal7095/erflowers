This policy explains how our website uses cookies and similar technologies, in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, work in a comparable way.

What we use

NamePurposeType
envie_cartRemembers the items in your bagStrictly necessary (local storage)
envie_ordersShows your recent orders on the Order History pageStrictly necessary (local storage)
envie_pending_orderKeeps your order details safe while you pay by cardStrictly necessary (local storage)

These are needed for features you ask for, such as your bag and checkout, so they do not require consent. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. If we introduce them in future, we will ask for your consent first.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: our fonts are loaded from Google, which receives your IP address so the fonts can be delivered to your browser.
  • Stripe: if you pay by card, Stripe sets cookies on its secure payment pages to process your payment and prevent fraud.
  • WhatsApp: if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing your browser data will empty your bag and remove your order history from this device.
