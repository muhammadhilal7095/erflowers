If the recipient is not in

If nobody is available, we may leave the roses with a neighbour or in a safe, sheltered place, or arrange another delivery. Please add any instructions, such as a safe place or concierge details, in the notes at checkout. Including the recipient’s phone number helps us deliver first time.

Busy periods

Valentine’s Day, Mothering Sunday and Christmas are our busiest times of year. We recommend ordering as early as possible. During these periods some designs may vary slightly in style or container while keeping the same look and value.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses

Please check the delivery address and postcode carefully. We cannot be responsible for failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete details, and a redelivery may need to be arranged.

Something not right?

If your roses arrive damaged, late or not as expected, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order reference and a photo. See our Refunds & Returns policy for how we put things right.
