Where we deliver

We deliver to addresses across the UK, including England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. For the Scottish Highlands and Islands, the Isle of Man, the Isles of Scilly and the Channel Islands, please message us before ordering so we can confirm availability and timings.

Choosing your delivery date

Choose your preferred delivery date and time slot at checkout. Enter the delivery postcode first and we’ll show the earliest date available for that address.

Need them today? Same-day delivery is available in selected areas when you order by (UK time). Use the postcode checker above to see if it’s available. For anywhere else, call us on or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll do our best.

Time slots are a preference, and we aim to deliver within the slot you choose. We are unable to guarantee an exact time unless we have agreed it with you in advance. If timing is important (for example a surprise or a proposal), please tell us in the notes at checkout or message us first.

If the recipient is not in

If nobody is available, we may leave the roses with a neighbour or in a safe, sheltered place, or arrange another delivery. Please add any instructions, such as a safe place or concierge details, in the notes at checkout. Including the recipient’s phone number helps us deliver first time.

Busy periods

Valentine’s Day, Mothering Sunday and Christmas are our busiest times of year. We recommend ordering as early as possible. During these periods some designs may vary slightly in style or container while keeping the same look and value.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses

Please check the delivery address and postcode carefully. We cannot be responsible for failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete details, and a redelivery may need to be arranged.

Something not right?

If your roses arrive damaged, late or not as expected, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order reference and a photo. See our Refunds & Returns policy for how we put things right.
