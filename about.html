At Envie Roses, we believe that flowers are more than just a gift. They are a language of love, a symbol of emotion and a way to create unforgettable moments.

Based in London, we design luxury rose boxes and bouquets for the moments that matter most: a first date, a proposal, an anniversary, a thank you, or simply because. Every arrangement is handcrafted with passion and care, using the finest blooms we can find.

From our signature black boxes to bespoke creations, our aim is simple: roses that look extraordinary, feel personal and arrive beautifully across the UK.
